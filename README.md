# Translayer

> A tool that reads your engineering work and turns it into plain English for the people who depend on it but don't speak code.

---

## 🛠️ The Problem

Engineers and business teams work on the same product but live in completely different worlds.

A data migration finishes. The engineer knows it took 3 weeks, touched 80M rows, had two near-misses, and involved decisions that will affect the system for years. The product manager knows: *"it's done."*

That gap causes bad decisions, misaligned priorities, and a slow breakdown of trust between the people building and the people depending on what's built.

Translayer automates the translation work so you don't have to manually summarize your progress every day.

---

## 🚀 What It Does

Point Translayer at your git history, migration logs, or PR descriptions and it generates a plain-English summary — human-readable context written for stakeholders who need to make decisions based on your work.
